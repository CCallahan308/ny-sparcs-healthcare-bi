# Limitations and caveats

Read before interpreting any figure in this system.

1. **Annual grain.** The de-identified source strips day and month. All series
   are annual. Nothing here implies daily, weekly, or monthly operational
   timing.
2. **Costs are estimates.** Total costs are DOH-derived estimates, not audited
   financials. Every cost figure is labeled "estimated."
3. **Charges are not reimbursement.** Total charges reflect billed amounts.
   They are not payment, net revenue, or reimbursement.
4. **Payer analysis is exposure, not margin.** No reimbursement amounts exist
   in the public file. Payer views show concentration of discharges and
   estimated cost, not profit or loss.
5. **No patient linkage.** De-identified public use data supports no
   readmission tracking, no longitudinal patient analysis, and no
   re-identification. This system adds none.
6. **Clinical grouping break.** Older years use CCS categories, recent years
   use CCSR. The system does not force them into one longitudinal service-line
   mapping. APR-DRG is the stable cross-year key.
7. **2020 assignable cause.** 2020 is a known disruption period (COVID-19
   pandemic response). Process-control baselines exclude 2020 by default while
   displayed series retain it, annotated.
8. **Redactions.** Some records (e.g., abortion-related discharges) have
   facility and geography fields redacted at source; counts by facility will
   not sum to statewide totals. Documented in the Data Quality page.
9. **Signals are not causes.** Control-chart signals are signals for
   investigation, potential special-cause variation. The system makes no
   causal claims.
